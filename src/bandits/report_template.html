    How to interpret these policies

    Stationary Thompson sampling keeps all past observations. Forgetting Thompson sampling discounts them. Blind hints combine sampled estimates with unchecked advice. Checked hints attenuate the whole advice source when evidence disagrees. The advice gate is a heuristic; this comparison does not establish a new algorithm or general superiority.
